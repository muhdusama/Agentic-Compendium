Settings

Appearance

Choose a display mode and accent colour. Changes sync between your devices.

Browser notifications

Get a private alert when new deliveries arrive, even with the app closed. Alerts contain no delivery titles or content. Enable separately on each device.

Checking this device…

On iPhone and iPad, add this app to your Home Screen and open it there first (iOS/iPadOS 16.4 or later). Browser and device notification settings control delivery.

Owner account

Change the Compendium username or password. All devices will need to sign in again after a change. Browser notifications will also be disabled on all devices.

Hermes connection

Hermes continues to own job schedules. Compendium only imports completed runs for the jobs you enable.

Portable archive

Download your deliveries, notes, channels and original imported archives. Credentials are excluded. Import a PWA archive (up to 256 MiB) or native app archive (up to 64 MiB); conflicting records are never overwritten. Keep downloads private.

Privacy & access

This PWA uses a private owner session, a single SQLite database and device-local offline cache. Sign out keeps this device's offline copy; use Forget this device on shared or lost devices to delete it. The native app is not replaced or deleted.
